Privacy policy

Last updated 28 July 2026

The short version

Your photos never leave your device. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. The analysis runs entirely inside your browser, and when you close the tab everything you gave it is gone.

Your photos and your camera

When you upload a photo or use your webcam, the image is decoded into a canvas in your browser and measured there. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, because there is no server to transmit it to: this site is a set of static files, with no backend, no database and no upload endpoint.

If you use the webcam, the video stream is read directly by your browser for live alignment feedback and stops the moment you close the camera panel. No frames are recorded unless you press Capture, and captured frames are held only in the page's memory.

Nothing is written to disk. The site does not use cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, or IndexedDB. Reloading or closing the page discards your photos, your answers and your report permanently. There is no history to delete, and no way for us to recover a report once the tab is closed.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses or accounts.
  • No cookies or similar storage, so no consent banner is needed.
  • No analytics, telemetry, session recording, fingerprinting or advertising pixels.
  • No photos, measurements, scores or reports. These exist only in your browser's memory.

Third parties your browser contacts

The site itself collects nothing, but loading it makes ordinary web requests to a few services. Any server you request a file from can see your IP address, the time of the request and basic details such as your browser version. We do not control those services and receive nothing back from them.

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) serves the two typefaces used across the site.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) serves the 3D library used by the landing page and the MediaPipe face-landmarking code used by the analysis.
  • Google Cloud Storage (storage.googleapis.com) serves the MediaPipe face-landmark model file, roughly a few megabytes, downloaded once and then cached by your browser. The model runs locally on your device; your photo is not sent to Google.

These are needed on first load. Once your browser has cached them, the analysis works without a network connection.

Hosting

This site is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like essentially all web hosts, the provider may keep short-lived server logs containing IP addresses and requested file paths for security and reliability purposes. Those logs are generated by the host's infrastructure, not by this site, and never contain your photos or your results, since those are never sent anywhere.

Children

This tool is not designed for children and is not directed at anyone under 13. Please do not use it to analyse photographs of children.

Photos of other people

Only use photographs you are entitled to use. Do not upload a photograph of another person without their clear and informed permission. Because processing is local, this is a matter of respecting that person rather than of data we hold, but it matters just as much.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Because there are no accounts, we cannot notify you directly, so please check back if this matters to you.

Contact
