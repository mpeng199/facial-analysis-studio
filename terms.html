The short version

This is an educational tool that estimates facial proportions from photographs. It is not medical advice, its numbers are estimates rather than measurements, and beauty is subjective. Use it with that in mind, and only on photographs you have the right to use.

What this tool is

Facial Analysis Studio measures landmark positions on a photograph of a face and scores them against published population norms, following the general method used by aesthetic analysis reports: landmark cephalometrics, four aesthetic tenets, and evidence-based non-surgical suggestions. It is an independent educational project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to QOVES or any clinic.

It is not medical advice

Nothing here is medical, dental, surgical, psychological or psychiatric advice, and no part of it is a diagnosis. It is not a medical device. Do not use it to make decisions about a procedure, a treatment or your health. Speak to a qualified professional for that, and never disregard professional advice because of something this tool produced.

If thinking about your appearance is causing you distress, please talk to a doctor or a mental health professional. A score from a web page is not a measure of your worth.

Accuracy and limits

Measurements are inferred from two-dimensional photographs. Camera lens, distance, angle, lighting, expression and hair all shift the result, and the underlying landmark detection has its own error. Scores are estimates with real uncertainty, not clinical measurements.

Norms differ by population, sex and age, and aesthetic standards differ by culture and change over time. The comparisons here reflect the specific reference data chosen for this project and the options you select. They are one perspective, not an objective verdict, and reasonable experts disagree about all of it.
